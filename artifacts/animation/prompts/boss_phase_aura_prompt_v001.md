## Prompt

Create one production-ready sprite sheet containing exactly three standalone
boss phase aura graphics for Kaboom Caravan, using the attached kawaii guardian
boss only as a style, palette, and core-shape reference. Do not draw the boss.

UI STYLE LOCK: stylized kawaii Japanese cartoon game art; cute mechanical
garage aesthetic; chunky rounded shapes; deep-navy clean outlines; coral, mint,
cyan, warm-yellow, and warm-ivory accents; soft toon shading; readable silhouette;
minimal surface noise. Cute but not childish. Mechanical but not realistic
military. No stone, rock, ruin, realistic rust, grime, photorealism, cyberpunk,
text, logo, or watermark.

PURPOSE: animated phase aura overlays centered on the glowing chest core of the
large living guardian boss. Each graphic must be a complete circular ring with a
large genuinely empty center so the core remains visible. The ring must rotate
cleanly around its exact visual center.

LAYOUT: exactly three isolated circular graphics in one horizontal row, evenly
spaced, identical center height and maximum diameter, with no overlap. Keep at
least 12 percent safe margin from the canvas edges and generous separation
between cells.

- Left / Phase 1: calm compact cyan-and-mint energy ring, simple rounded shield
  segments, lowest intensity and fewest details.
- Middle / Phase 2: expanded cyan-and-mint mechanical ring with small coral
  directional fins and stronger energy arcs, clearly more active than Phase 1.
- Right / Phase 3: overdrive ring with warm-yellow and coral rotating blades,
  deep-navy hardware, cyan core accents, strongest silhouette and intensity;
  energetic but still kawaii and clean.

OUTPUT: square high-resolution sprite sheet. Flat solid chroma-key magenta
`#ff00ff` background across the entire canvas, including every ring center and
all gaps. No shadows outside the assets, no labels, no panel, no scene, no
screen mockup, no extra objects, and no readable text.
