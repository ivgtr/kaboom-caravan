# Character Motion Sheet Prompts v002

## Shared style lock

Stylized kawaii cartoon game character motion sheet for Kaboom Caravan. Japanese
game-inspired 2D art. Kawaii 65 percent, mechanical garage 25 percent, dangerous
combat energy 10 percent. Rounded chunky silhouette, warm ivory, deep navy,
coral, mint, cyan, warm yellow and soft lavender. Thick clean deep-navy outline,
soft toon shading, broad readable shapes, maximum three major materials, minimal
surface noise. Cute but not childish. Bright, playful and toy-like rather than
grim or realistic. No photorealism, no realistic military styling, no cyberpunk,
no readable text, no logo, no watermark.

Create one square 2 by 2 motion sheet. The four cells must show exactly the same
character with identical face, proportions, palette, outline width, lighting,
scale and ground baseline. No panel borders and no labels. Leave at least 10
percent safe margin inside every cell. Flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background.
No cast shadow outside the feet or wheels. Do not crop any part of the character.

Cell order:

1. top-left: neutral idle pose with firm ground contact
2. top-right: locomotion contact pose, body weight visibly transferred
3. bottom-left: attack anticipation pose with a readable wind-up
4. bottom-right: attack release or recoil pose, character still intact

Do not add impact flashes, projectiles, smoke clouds or speed lines; those are
rendered separately by the game.

## Player Caravan

Face right. A compact adorable armored caravan, coral-and-warm-ivory rounded body,
deep-navy chassis and two large wheels, mint cabin window, small cyan garage
lights. Keep the roof and side hardpoints visibly clear for dynamic weapons and
modules; generate no mounted weapon. The silhouette must remain a vehicle, not a
creature. Idle wheels grounded; movement frame rotates wheel spokes and compresses
the rear suspension; anticipation frame leans slightly forward and braces; release
frame squats under a large weapon recoil without showing the weapon. Preserve the
same wheelbase and hardpoint locations in every cell. No rocks, rust or grime.

## Basic / モスモコ

Face left. A small plush moss creature with a round mint body, cream face, tiny
navy feet and two soft leaf tufts. Friendly-looking but determined. A few smooth
pebble freckles may occupy no more than 8 percent of the design; it must not look
made of rocks. Locomotion uses alternating foot squash. Anticipation compresses
sideways and lowers its brow. Release is a short rounded body-bump pose.

## Rusher / ハナツノ

Face left. A low four-legged coral creature with a single rounded flower-bud horn,
cream muzzle, deep-navy hooves and a mint leaf scarf. Smooth organic body with no
metal washer parts and no stone armor. Locomotion is a strong front-leg contact.
Anticipation lowers the flower horn and shifts weight backward. Release is a
grounded charge pose with legs extended.

## Heavy / ガレキガメ

Face left. A large gentle-looking indigo-and-turquoise tortoise guardian. Its broad
domed shell resembles one smooth rounded garage safety panel or glazed ceramic
shell, not a rubble pile. Add only two or three tiny warm-ivory old-world token
plates, together below 15 percent of the silhouette. No masonry blocks, jagged
rocks, realistic cracks or dirt. Locomotion shifts weight onto one thick foot.
Anticipation retracts the head slightly and braces the shell. Release is a heavy
grounded shell slam pose.

## Artillery / ホウシダケ

Face left. A smooth lavender-and-mint mushroom creature with a large soft rounded
cap, cream stalk-body, tiny feet and three cyan spore pods. The cap is clean and
rubbery, with no stones, rubble or realistic fungus texture. Locomotion is a small
root-foot shuffle. Anticipation compresses the cap downward while the cyan pods
brighten. Release flips the cap upward as if launching spores, without drawing
the spores as projectiles.

## Bomber / バクレツミ

Face left. A round orange-and-coral fruit creature with a cream belly, tiny navy
feet, warm-yellow seed lights and a mint leaf fuse tuft. Smooth peel-like surface,
no stone armor and no realistic cracks. Locomotion is a waddling contact pose.
Anticipation swells into a wider round silhouette with tense eyes. Release snaps
forward as if ejecting explosive energy; keep the character whole and do not show
an explosion.

## Boss / Kawaii Fortress

Face left. A colossal rounded biological guardian, primarily soft lavender, warm
ivory and coral, with a readable expressive face and four broad grounded limbs.
The living creature must occupy at least 70 percent of the visual identity. Add
small smooth ivory old-world garage plates and cyan energy cores as accessories,
together below 15 percent of the silhouette. It must not resemble a castle,
tower, masonry golem or rubble mound. No stone blocks, crenellations, jagged rocks
or realistic cracks. Locomotion is a weighty front-foot stomp contact. Anticipation
plants all feet and charges the cyan cores. Release pushes the chest/core forward
with a massive but rounded recoil pose.
